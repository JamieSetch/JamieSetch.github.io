Hi, I'm Jamie Setch, an MPhil student in NeuroAI & Intelligent Systems at the University of Cambridge. I'm interested in developing brain-inspired efficient AI infrastructure. Outside of my studies I continue to lead the development of nerve, a no-code spiking neural network IDE for edge AI simulation and deployment. Prior to this I studied Psychology at Newcastle University, focusing on neural processing and biologically plausible RL.
