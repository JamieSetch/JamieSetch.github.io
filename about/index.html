Hi! I'm Jamie Setch, an MPhil student at the University of Cambridge working at the meeting point of neuroscience and AI. I build spiking neural network tools and brain–computer interfaces, and I'm most interested in brain-inspired control for robots, reinforcement learning and AI safety.

Feel free to get in touch about research, a possible collaboration, or anything interesting.

Current

  • MPhil in Cognitive Neuroscience (NeuroAI & Intelligent Systems) at the University of Cambridge, 2026–27.
  • Founder and lead developer of NERVE, where a team of four engineers and I are building nervelabs, a spiking neural network IDE for robotic control.
  • Self-studying Imperial College London's Computer Science course, currently on the Year 3 modules in NLP, Deep Learning and Robotics.

Background

  • BSc Psychology, Newcastle University (2023–2026), with four research placements.
  • Summer research intern at the Max Planck Institute (2025), modelling biological movement with reinforcement learning.
  • President and Alumni Officer of the Newcastle University Neuroscience & Cognition Society, and a peer mentor.
  • Finalist, Undergraduate of the Year in AI & Technology (Ocado, 2024).
  • Outside the lab: Ironman finisher (2023) and a rower with eight national records.
