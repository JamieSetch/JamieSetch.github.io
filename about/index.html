Hi, I'm Jamie, an MPhil student in NeuroAI & Intelligent Systems at the University of Cambridge, where I'm focused on developing brain-inspired efficient AI infrastructure. Outside of my studies I continue to lead the development of nerve, a no-code spiking neural network IDE for edge AI simulation and deployment. Prior to this I studied Psychology at Newcastle University, focusing on neural processing and biologically plausible RL.

Outside of academia I play originals with my band across London and Cambridge, and run seehimal.com with my good friend Rabi. I'm always open to new ideas or adventures, be they mountaineering, diving, or building something cool. Drop me an email if you have a plan in mind: jamiebensetch@icloud.com.
